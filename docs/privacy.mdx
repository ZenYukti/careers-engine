# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to ZenYukti Jobs, a service that distributes career opportunities through Discord and Telegram. The service is operated by ZenYukti, with Careers Engine providing the underlying infrastructure for processing and distributing opportunities.

This policy describes the information handled in connection with the service, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Process

The information processed depends on how you interact with ZenYukti Jobs and the platforms through which it operates.

**Platform and interaction data**

When you interact with a bot or use platform features, Discord or Telegram may make information such as your platform user ID, username, chat or server identifiers, messages, and interaction details available to the service. The information available depends on the interaction and the permissions granted to the bot.

**Career opportunity data**

Careers Engine processes career listings that may include company names, job titles, locations, application links, and other opportunity-related information. This is distinct from users' personal information.

**Technical and operational data**

Technical records, delivery responses, and operational logs may be processed to diagnose failures, maintain reliability, and secure the service.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with ZenYukti Jobs may be used to:

- Publish and distribute career opportunities through Discord and Telegram.
- Respond to direct interactions with the bots, where supported.
- Maintain service reliability, security, and performance.
- Diagnose technical issues and investigate misuse.
- Respond to support requests and privacy enquiries.

Information is not used for unrelated purposes unless disclosed or permitted by applicable law.

## 3. Platform Providers

ZenYukti Jobs operates through third-party platforms, including:

- [Discord](https://discord.com/privacy)
- [Telegram](https://telegram.org/privacy)

These platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms. ZenYukti does not control their independent data-handling practices.

The service may also rely on hosting, automation, or other infrastructure providers to operate Careers Engine. Such providers may process information where necessary to provide or maintain the service.

## 4. Data Sharing

ZenYukti does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the service, or disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Career listings may link to external websites. If you visit those websites or submit an application, the relevant third party's privacy policy and practices apply to the information you provide.

## 5. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention depends on the type of information, its operational purpose, and the system in which it is maintained. Technical records may be retained for troubleshooting, security, and service maintenance.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the service against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 7. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may stop using the service, leave the relevant Discord server or Telegram channel, or stop interacting with the bots at any time.

For questions or requests concerning information associated with your interactions, contact us using the details below. Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable law and the information available to us.

## 8. Children's Privacy

ZenYukti Jobs is intended for people seeking career opportunities and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to a bot, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the service, its data-handling practices, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published at this URL, with the date of the most recent update.
